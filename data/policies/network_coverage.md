# Network Coverage Policy

This is a synthetic policy created for the HealthOps ClaimGuard AI demonstration and does not represent CMS, Evernorth, Cigna, or any real payer policy.

## NC-01 Purpose
This demonstration policy guides review of claims involving provider network status and benefit coverage signals.

## NC-02 Out-of-Network Indicator
Out-of-network claims should be reviewed for member benefit design, referral requirements, emergency exceptions, and documented network exceptions.

## NC-03 Coverage Verification
Coverage-active signals should be checked against eligibility records for the service date before any final analyst recommendation.

## NC-04 Review Procedure
When network status contributes to risk, analysts should verify provider participation, service location, and any exception notes in the source system.

# Timely Filing Policy

This is a synthetic policy created for the HealthOps ClaimGuard AI demonstration and does not represent CMS, Evernorth, Cigna, or any real payer policy.

## TF-01 Purpose
This demonstration policy describes review steps for timely filing and late submission signals.

## TF-02 Filing Window
Claims submitted outside the expected filing window should be reviewed for corrected-claim status, coordination-of-benefits delays, disaster exceptions, or documented extensions.

## TF-03 Late Filing Signal
A timely filing flag indicates that elapsed time or claim sequence patterns may require analyst review.

## TF-04 Review Procedure
Analysts should verify original receipt date, corrected claim indicators, payer extension notes, and service date before disposition.

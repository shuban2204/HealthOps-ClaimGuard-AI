# Duplicate Claims Policy

This is a synthetic policy created for the HealthOps ClaimGuard AI demonstration and does not represent CMS, Evernorth, Cigna, or any real payer policy.

## DUP-01 Purpose
This demonstration policy supports unusual duplicate-claim review.

## DUP-02 Duplicate Indicators
Potential duplicates may share beneficiary, provider, service date, procedure family, and similar claim amount patterns.

## DUP-03 Duplicate Claim Signal
A duplicate flag should prompt source-system verification. The signal alone is not an automatic claim denial or fraud finding.

## DUP-04 Review Procedure
Analysts should compare claim identifiers, line details, service dates, and any adjustment history before closing the duplicate review item.

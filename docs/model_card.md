# Model Card

## Intended Use
Prioritize synthetic healthcare claims for analyst review by estimating denial risk and surfacing operational drivers.

## Data
The training data is generated by `ml/generate_data.py`. It contains no real patient, provider, or payer records.

## Model
The risk model is a scikit-learn `HistGradientBoostingClassifier`. The unusual-claim detector is an `IsolationForest`.

## Metrics
Metrics are written to `ml/artifacts/metrics.json` by `ml/train.py`, including ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, F1, selected threshold, confusion matrix, and precision at the top 10 percent of scored claims.

## Limitations
The model learns from synthetic label rules and should not be interpreted as production performance. Outputs are decision support only and require human review.
